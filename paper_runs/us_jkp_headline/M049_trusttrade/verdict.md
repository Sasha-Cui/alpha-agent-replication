# M049: TrustTrade selective-consensus trading

Status: **closed not evaluable on monthly U.S./JKP data**.

TrustTrade's central mechanism is the composition of multi-agent selective consensus, deterministic temporal anchoring, sequential trader/risk-manager decisions, and reflective memory. The paper does not identify enough of any of these interfaces to generate a score: agent count/model settings and report traces, claim-extraction schema, embedding model, consensus thresholds/weights, temporal polynomial and action rules, reflection prompts/models, memory window, and final 2026 decisions are absent.

The three paper-linked Netlify interfaces are meaningful author-attributable software, but only for the human study. They implement the six-stage display/annotation workflow and deterministic $10,000 whole-share 25/50/75/100% transaction widget, not TrustTrade's selective-consensus pipeline. No participant outputs are public. Their static inputs are also unsuitable as causal evidence: 68/183 nominal-2024 stock-days contain later-year references, including strong realized 2025/2026 facts, and four reports retain a final transaction proposal.

JKP supplies numerical monthly histories but cannot reconstruct missing report claims, embeddings, consensus credibility, temporal score, or memory-conditioned action. The inactive baseline table has no unique executable convention and is not the proposed system. No return path is fabricated.

This closure does not prove the paper's graphical performance claims are false; it records that no causal TrustTrade decision policy or auditable forward-run output is public. Reopen for an attributable pipeline and decision/holding lineage.
