# M061: AGORA agent-to-agent self-evolution

Status: **closed not evaluable on monthly U.S./JKP data**.

AGORA's central strategy is the output of a five-role agent society that evolves AlphaGen programs and evaluation metrics, promotes them through per-round registries, and relays a selected top-30 pool into an incrementally trained MaskablePPO trading policy. The paper identifies a dynamic CSI1000 universe, five-day target and rebalance, decile shell, and next-open execution, but the score and actions come from unreleased artifacts.

The strongest executable evidence is real but non-trading: two complete printed metric programs execute deterministically on controlled panels. They score already-supplied predictions or drawdowns; neither creates an alpha, selects the pool, or emits an action. The contribution section claims code, baselines, leakage audit, and registries are released, while Appendix A.8 says they will be released. Neither the PDF nor source gives a repository URL, and fresh exact repository and registry-code searches remain empty.

Missing items include the market snapshot, 94 alpha programs, top-30 pool and signs, prompts/model calls, registries, PPO environments and checkpoints, actions, holdings, returns, and raw result arrays. JKP cannot infer those programs or policy actions. Substituting generic JKP factors would bypass AGORA's defining agent-to-agent self-evolution.

No return path is fabricated. None of 293 table cells or four empirical panels was regenerated author-natively. This closure does not establish that the paper's performance claims are false, although several internal numeric and transaction-cost conflicts make raw code and arrays especially necessary.
