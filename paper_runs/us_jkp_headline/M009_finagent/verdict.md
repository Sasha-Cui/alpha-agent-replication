# M009: FinAgent common-task verdict

Status: **closed—not evaluable on the monthly U.S./JKP task without replacing the multimodal FinAgent decision policy**.

FinAgent’s headline strategy combines market-intelligence summaries, price charts, three-level memory and diversified retrieval, low- and high-level reflection, strategy tools, and a final BUY/HOLD/SELL decision. This is a real, substantial author-linked release: 65 paper-era core modules import, 13 of 31 audited mechanisms are present, and controlled environment and metric components execute deterministically.

The executable pieces do not supply the strategy’s decisions. The exact Financial Modeling Prep snapshot, paper-time news and fundamentals, rendered decision images, memories, trajectories, model outputs, actions, equity paths, checkpoints, trials, and result tables are absent. The native validation chart also exposes 14 future days, which cannot be used under the common no-lookahead contract. The paper defines independent daily single-asset decisions rather than a monthly cross-sectional portfolio for 1,000 stocks.

The released Buy-and-Hold and technical rules are comparators/tools, not FinAgent. The 90 rule records match 0 of 288 comparable appendix cells, while the current-Yahoo Buy-and-Hold replay matches 13 of 36 unique cells but has neither paper-time input lineage nor FinAgent-result credit. A controlled synthetic BUY/HOLD/SELL sequence checks accounting only. Promoting any of these, or building a reduced modern-model policy from JKP characteristics, would replace the headline mechanism.

No monthly return is assigned. Unlike Alpha-GPT 2.0, FinAgent does report positive empirical results; however, zero of its 1,061 published result units is reproduced from a native agent run. That is an unresolved replication failure, not evidence that the paper’s claims are false and not evidence that it merely loses to JKP.

M009 is closed without a return. M010, LLMFactor, is now active.
