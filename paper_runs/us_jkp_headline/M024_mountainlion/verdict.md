# M024: MountainLion common-task verdict

Status: **closed—not evaluable as a monthly U.S./JKP strategy**.

MountainLion combines four crypto agents, OHLCV windows, historical/real-time news, sentiment, GraphRAG and Perplexity retrieval, LLM and ML forecast tracks, adaptive fusion, reflection, and feedback. Two attributable repositories contain genuine product components: the locked frontend builds reproducibly, the paper-time platform core compiles, and appendix endpoints plus the ten-token menu correspond exactly.

Those repositories do not generate the paper’s forecasts. The only prediction-related backend path reads three already-computed rows from private MySQL state. No training panel, split, estimator, fitted model, cross-validation protocol, GraphRAG snapshot, runtime prompt/request/response, prediction/target array, adaptive-alpha implementation, or result runner exists in 2,273 reachable commits. Private modules, service state, and credentials are also absent.

More fundamentally, the paper reports forecast CV/MSE but no executable portfolio, holdings, actions, fills, costs, returns, or benchmark. Running new prompts, implementing the fusion equation alone, inferring signals from figures, or mapping crypto inputs to JKP would invent the strategy. No return is assigned.

Zero of 20 forecasting-performance cells and none of six material quantitative claims is faithfully regenerated. The abstract’s improved-return claim has no reported return object to reproduce. M024 is therefore closed as non-evaluable rather than classified as false or as JKP underperformance. M025, ContestTrade, is now active.
