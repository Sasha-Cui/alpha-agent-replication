# M033: GuruAgents Buffett score on monthly U.S./JKP data

Status: **completed partial evaluation**, not a native replay of the GPT-4o GuruAgent or the paper's 2023-2025 backtest.

The paper-highlighted Warren Buffett sleeve was selected before the new run because the abstract names it as the best GuruAgent (reported 42.2% CAGR). The implementation executes the official source prompt's disclosed quality-value arithmetic: 5th/95th-percentile winsorization, min-max scaling, the stated valuation/base/QualityPlus terms, raw bonuses and penalties, clipping, score rounding, and score-proportional long-only weights. Fourteen source metrics are mapped to direct or algebraically reconstructed JKP ratios. Margin stability and buyback yield are omitted because the available fields do not match the source windows, with the remaining positive QualityPlus weights renormalized as frozen in the recipe.

At the common 10 bp one-way cost, the complete August 1999-December 2024 path has CAGR **7.93%**, annualized Sharpe **0.532**, and maximum drawdown **-51.35%**. Over the 185-month attribution window, the rolling JKP133 residual mean is **+0.65% annually** (HAC t=**1.364**, two-sided p=**0.1725**; descriptive 69-test bound=1.0000). At the paper's stated 1 bp cost, CAGR is 8.09% and Sharpe 0.541. The deliberately severe missing-return sensitivity—assigning every missing held return a position-adverse 100% move—falls to 2.22% CAGR, 0.215 Sharpe, and a -4.23% annual residual; primary missing-return gross weight averages 0.45% and peaks at 2.03%.

This is a source-anchored central partial adaptation, not a full-paper replication. The original system lets GPT-4o perform arithmetic and table generation, rebalances quarterly on a static NASDAQ-100 file, rounds weights to whole percentages, uses input quarters without filing dates, and does not actually apply its declared 1 bp cost. The common study instead executes the intended formula deterministically on point-in-time JKP rows, rebalances monthly in the largest 1,000 U.S. stocks, uses continuous weights, and charges 10 bp. The other four gurus, persona rationales, meta-agent, and paper result paths are outside this milestone.

Bottom line: the Buffett idea yields a positive long-only return on JKP, but the broad benchmark absorbs most of it. This is closer to option **B** than **A**: we implemented a defensible disclosed component and it does not show significant residual alpha; it does not reproduce or validate the paper's reported 42.2% native result.
