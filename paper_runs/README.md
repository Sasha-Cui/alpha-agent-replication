manifest, and completion receipt determine what it supports. A completed
historical package does not complete the reopened monthly investment study.

## Reading a result

Find the declared variant, exact source and input identities, chronological
split/decision clock, model or policy, portfolio rule, and cost convention.
Then check terminal success, completion and replay/account receipts, the actual
return calendar, benchmark identity, and stated claim boundary.

A queued job, partial source, reproduced chart, passing test, or finite processed
matrix is not by itself a completed investment experiment. Retain failed and
unavailable cases alongside successful ones.

Historical claim and runner details are indexed in
[the experiment catalog](../docs/EXPERIMENT_INDEX.md).
