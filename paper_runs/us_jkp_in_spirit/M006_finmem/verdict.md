# M006: FinMem in-spirit reconstruction on monthly U.S./JKP data

Status: **completed researcher-authored in-spirit reconstruction**, not a native FinMem replication.

The reconstruction preserves FinMem's shallow, intermediate, and deep memory layers; top-five retrieval; layer-specific recency, relevance, and importance scoring; reflection over current facts and memory votes; and an adaptive risk character. Numeric JKP states and cosine similarity replace unavailable news/filing text and embeddings. Only earlier security outcomes enter memories, and the trailing three-month formation-time market return switches between risk-seeking and risk-averse modes. The unavailable GPT-4-Turbo reflections, trained memory stream, and native action histories are not reproduced.

At 10 bp one-way costs, the 305-month path has CAGR -2.63%, annualized Sharpe 0.024, and maximum drawdown -91.81%. Mean monthly traded notional is 2.778, and minimum signal coverage is 904 stocks. Character months are risk_seeking=231, risk_averse=74.

Across the 185-month rolling JKP attribution window, residual mean return is 1.39% annually (HAC t=0.397, p=0.6911, 95% interval [-5.45%, 8.23%]).

This result answers how one transparent FinMem-inspired layered-memory policy transfers to the common task. It does not reproduce or validate the paper's private memories, LLM decisions, five-trial paths, reported returns, or model-comparison claims.
