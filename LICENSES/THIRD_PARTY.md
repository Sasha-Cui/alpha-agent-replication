# Third-party materials

This project does not relicense third-party papers, repositories, model
weights, prompts, hosted services, or market data. URLs, commit identifiers,
observed license identifiers, and bounded metadata are recorded for research
audit and citation. An observed license of `NOASSERTION` means that the audit
did not establish a reusable license; it does not mean that the material is in
the public domain.

The JKP Global Factor Data inputs remain subject to their source terms. Raw
Parquet files and generated security-level return panels are not distributed
as part of the paper package. The release contains code, hashes, aggregate
statistics, and provenance sufficient for an authorized user to rebuild the
analysis from separately obtained inputs.
