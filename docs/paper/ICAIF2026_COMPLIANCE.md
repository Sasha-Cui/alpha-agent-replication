# ICAIF 2026 submission compliance

Official source: <https://icaif2026.org/call-for-papers.html>, checked
2026-08-08. The deadline is 2026-08-09 at 23:59 Anywhere on Earth.

## Manuscript-controlled requirements

- [x] PDF submission.
- [x] Latest production ACM article template: `acmart` 2.19, 2026-06-27.
- [x] `sigconf` two-column format with `anonymous` parameter.
- [x] At most eight total pages, including figures and references.
- [x] Self-contained manuscript.
- [x] No appendix or supplementary material.
- [x] Double-blind title block and PDF metadata.
- [x] Self-references, if any, use ordinary third-person citations.
- [x] Valid CCS concepts, keywords, figure descriptions, and ACM numeric bibliography.
- [x] Conference name, dates, year, and location match the official call.
- [x] All fonts embedded; US Letter page size; no unresolved references or overfull boxes.
- [x] AI use that affected research organization, code, diagnostics, and prose is disclosed in the methods/audit-trail text; the human author accepts responsibility.

The executable check is `python scripts/validate_icaif2026_format.py`.

## Author/CMT confirmations required before upload

These policies cannot be proven from the manuscript and must be confirmed by
the submitting author in CMT:

- The CMT author list and order are complete and final.
- No author exceeds ICAIF's twelve-submission limit.
- The work is not under review, accepted, or published at another archival venue,
  and will not be submitted elsewhere during ICAIF review.
- Any prior version is only a permissible technical report or non-archival workshop
  version and is not cited in a way that breaks anonymity.
- All authors agree to perform a light review load if requested.
- At least one author will present in person if accepted.
- Every author will have an ORCID before camera-ready preparation.
