# Current research objective (2026-09-03)

The user has reoriented this project to a bounded, paper-by-paper comparison of
headline trading strategies on monthly U.S. stocks using the JKP data and
benchmark. Read `docs/US_JKP_HEADLINE_PROTOCOL.md` and
`paper_runs/us_jkp_headline/milestones.json` before continuing research.

- Work on one of the 69 paper milestones at a time.
- Extract the central proposed strategy from the paper or its code. Preserve its
  economic/algorithmic mechanism while documenting the U.S./monthly/JKP adapter.
- Evaluate the best defensible implementation or central partial implementation;
  do not require recreation of every original result, ablation, seed, or API.
- A negative or insignificant JKP result is a completed experiment, not a reason
  to search for a more favorable specification.
- Close a genuinely non-evaluable paper with concrete evidence and a bounded
  effort record, then move on. Do not manufacture a generic factor proxy and
  attribute it to the paper.
- Reuse existing source audits. Do not restart exhaustive fork/history searches,
  paper-layout reconstruction, or all-table conformance unless a specific missing
  item is necessary for the active headline implementation.
- Keep milestone closure, evaluated headline strategies, evaluated partial
  strategies, and unavailable cases as separate counts. Historical "full paper
  reproduced" flags and unit-test totals are not the new progress measure.
- Preserve old studies and claim boundaries as provenance. Do not relabel old
  proxies, checkpoints, or author-output verification as new strategy results.
- All real work and execution remain on Bouchet in this canonical checkout.
  Keep licensed JKP security-level data and derived holdings out of public Git.

The September 3 user direction supersedes the earlier open-ended requirement to
reproduce every original paper experiment before moving to the next paper.
